MISSING CHILD RESPONSE STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)

Version: 1.0
Organization: RescueNet-AI Command Center

1. Objective
Provide a standardized response for missing child incidents.

2. Immediate Response
- Register complaint immediately.
- Generate unique Case ID.
- Assign status: OPEN.
- Notify nearest police station.
- Record child and guardian details.

3. Risk Priority
CRITICAL:
- Child below 10 years
- Kidnapping suspected
- Medical emergency
- Human trafficking suspicion

HIGH:
- Missing > 6 hours
- Public crowded area

MEDIUM:
- Missing < 6 hours
- No immediate danger

LOW:
- Voluntary absence suspected

4. Investigation Workflow
1. Verify complaint
2. Geocode address
3. Search CCTV
4. AI Face Recognition
5. Notify Police & NGOs
6. Broadcast Alerts
7. Update Dashboard

5. Case Status
OPEN
IN_PROGRESS
RECOVERED
CLOSED

6. Dashboard Metrics
- Total Cases
- Open Cases
- Investigation Cases
- Recovered Cases
- Closed Cases

7. AI Modules
- Face Recognition
- Risk Prediction
- RAG Knowledge Base
- Multilingual Chatbot
- Speech-to-Text
- Text-to-Speech
- Geospatial Analytics

8. Emergency Numbers
Police: 100
Child Helpline: 1098

9. Citizen Guidelines
Upload a recent photo.
Provide accurate address.
Mention last seen location.
Keep contact number active.

End of Document.
